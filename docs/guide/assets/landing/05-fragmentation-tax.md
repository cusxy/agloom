## Content

**Eyebrow:** `THE TAX YOU'RE PAYING`

**H2:** Every assistant wants its own format. We're maintaining all of them.

**Body:**

> Modern engineering teams rarely standardise on a single AI assistant. One developer prefers Claude, another Codex, the new hire installed Gemini last week. Each tool reads its own instruction file, its own skills directory, its own settings format. The longer this goes on, the more they drift apart — and the more time your team spends keeping them in sync by hand.
